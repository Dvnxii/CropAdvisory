Integrated Pest Management (IPM) prioritizes cultural and biological controls before chemical intervention: crop rotation, resistant varieties, field sanitation, pheromone traps for monitoring, and conserving natural predators (ladybird beetles, spiders, parasitic wasps).

Economic threshold levels (ETL) should guide spray decisions rather than spraying preventively on a calendar. Spraying below ETL wastes input cost and accelerates resistance development in pest populations.

Weather matters for spray timing: avoid spraying before forecast rain (within 6-8 hours) since most contact and systemic pesticides need a dry window to be effective, and avoid spraying during high wind (above ~15 km/h) to prevent drift.
